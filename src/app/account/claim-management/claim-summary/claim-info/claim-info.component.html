Changes to Claims Servicing

We are transitioning our claims servicing to AIG

As you're aware, we have recently entered into an agreement with AIG, and beginning October 22, 2021, AIG will be responsible for processing the majority of the service requests under HWA-branded plans. You may still receive sporadic work requests from HWA as we complete the transition.

Submitting Invoices

For claims opened prior to October 22, 2021, please submit all invoices within 30 days of completing the work. We strongly encourage you to submit your invoices as soon as possible to ensure you're paid in a timely manner.

Additional Opportunities

AIG may need your expertise and experience to service claims on HWA-branded plans. If you are interested in continuing to service the HWA customer base as an independent service provider for AIG, please reach out to the AIG team at warranty_recruitment@aig.com.

Here are some potential advantages to working with AIG:

  • AIG uses the Service Power Application, which makes it easy to update claim statuses and bill out for completed jobs
  • AIG call agents handle the appointment booking for service providers during the claims filing process
  • In many cases, Trade Call Fees are collected by AIG agents during the claims filing process so you will no longer have to collect it from those customers

If you are an existing AIG preferred provider or have already submitted your application, you need take no further action at this time.

Thank you for your support in serving our customers.
